# vgpu 0.6.0 — research candidate

Released October 12, 2026. MIT licensed. Its WGSL shader reflection, GPU device-loss handling, scene hierarchy and explicit render/compute frame ownership could inform a future WebGPU rendering backend.

Source: https://github.com/vercel-labs/vgpu/releases/tag/v0.6.0

Milestones: M2/M4. Windows, Linux and macOS need a compatible browser and WebGPU implementation. No integration until ModernGL-to-display transport is measured.
