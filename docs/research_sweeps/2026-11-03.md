## Native integration this week: experimental tempo-locked breathing

Inspired by the **2026-11-03 Fosfora tempo-LFO/binding work**, implemented a deliberately smaller independent Python/ModernGL-compatible effect on our *existing* Performance Director rather than pulling Rust/wgpu, TouchDesigner or upstream code.

- `src/projection_mapping/performance_director.py`: `_bar_breath(signals, amount)` is a zero-centered, once-per-bar cosine derived only from published `MusicalSignals.bar_phase`, with a confidence gate and a neutral fallback when the clock is missing/invalid. It modulates shader intensity (±12%), particle bloom (±16%) and field force (±10%), never MADNESS or cue identity. Default amount is 0: existing looks remain unchanged.
- `experiments/33_performance_director.py`: `--tempo-breath` parameter. PerformanceDirector now receives `clock_signals` rather than raw audio `signals` so optional Ableton Link's clock phase controls the breath without replacing audio envelope/event data.
- `configs/features.d/performance_director.toml`: 0..1 slider in Behavior & Reactivity, default 0. 
- `tests/test_performance_director.py`: phase/wrap/invalid-clock tests, default unchanged, on/off energy modulation, MADNESS isolation.
- `docs/PERFORMANCE_CONTROL.md`, `docs/feature_tracks/audio_visual.md`, `ROADMAP.md`: operator instructions and incubating status.

The built-in capture/phase estimator is heuristic and can half/double count. This effect does not claim sub-frame downbeat precision. No independent wall-clock oscillator, per-frame binding string allocations, or new dependencies were introduced.

**Validation boundary:** standalone numerical checks of the cosine/confidence rule passed. New repository unit tests are committed but **were not executed** in a checkout during the sweep; they are not reported as passed. GPU/GLSL was not invoked. No live audio/MIDI/Link, projector display, brightness, latency or frame-time claims are validated.
