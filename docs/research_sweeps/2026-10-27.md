### Attractor Lab flowing illumination — experimental implementation

Source `src/projection_mapping/attractor_lab.py`, standalone experiment `experiments/24_attractor_lab.py`, `configs/features.d/attractor_lab.toml`, `tests/test_attractor_lab.py` and the mathematical-worlds track were updated. For continuous ODE modes (Lorenz, Rossler, Aizawa, Thomas, Halvorsen), three staggered heads advance across the precomputed ordered RK4 samples with fading luminous tails. The previously static point cloud should now reveal *directional flow* as the light segments move. This is animated illumination along a fixed sample path, NOT per-frame ODE integration and NOT independent particle advective state; finite trajectory endpoints are darkened to reduce visible looping teleports. Discrete 2D iterative maps (Clifford, De Jong, Ikeda) retain full point-field rendering because neighboring iterations are not geometrically adjacent.

Material art pass: narrower, less-white point cores; vivid cyan/magenta shells; bright-only bloom threshold; peak-luminance display compression preserving RGB channel ratios instead of independent per-channel desaturation. Controls: `--flow-speed` 0–8 (phase advances 1 cycle per 24 seconds at 1.0), `--flow-width` 0.01–0.25 (fraction of trajectory per luminous window). Defaults: 1.0, 0.08. Existing ModernGL/GLSL 330 stack; no new dependencies.

Validation: numerical and shader-source **tests were added**; they were not run against a checkout in this remote run. A stand-alone Python compilation/numerical sanity check of the phase helper passed (wrap, speed, invalid floats, active segment fraction), but this does **not** test the checked-in full renderer or compile GLSL. No OpenGL compile/render, visual comparison, projector test, GPU performance or camera/projector latency has been verified. Compare at 5/30/60 s under a real OpenGL context and examine the 24-second wrap transition for flicker. Failure to meet the art direction bar should be treated as incubation, not as finished visual.
